Step 1 · Find the accounts that use your old address

Adressumzug looks through the old mailbox for welcome mails, password resets and login alerts. Each sender is probably a website where you have an account. It only reads the sender, subject and date, and the text of a few welcome mails. Nothing leaves this computer.
